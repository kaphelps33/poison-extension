Difficulty

Select how miserable your watching experience will be!

  1. Videos are in black and white, short form content is blocked!

  2. All disruptions from easy plus no more recommended videos on the home screen or after a video! No more comments either!

  3. All disruptions from easy and devoted plus a filter over the video that makes the audio unpleasant to hear!

Caution!

Upon selecting a mode you cannot undo it without passing a test! You can move up in modes without a test, but you cannot move down!
